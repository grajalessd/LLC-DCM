Terms of Service

LLC DCM · Cargo & Transport · Houston, Texas

  1. Booking

    Appointments are booked by WhatsApp, 2 to 3 days in advance. Please include a photo of your items, the pickup and drop-off addresses, and the date you need.

  2. Quotes

    Each quote covers the full trip and is based on mileage, fuel and the items being transported.

  3. Payment

    Payment is due once your items are loaded in the van, before the trip begins. We accept Zelle and Apple Pay. No deposit is required.

  4. Cancellations

    You can cancel at no cost. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice before your scheduled pickup so we can plan our route.

  5. Packing

    Items must be packed and ready for pickup. We wrap assembled furniture in moving paper. We do not assemble or disassemble furniture.

  6. Limits

    Each item can weigh up to 300 lbs. The total load can't exceed 80 cubic feet.

  7. Items we don't transport

    Animals, jewelry, chemicals, large mirrors, refrigerated items, stone, long lumber, cement and food. We also don't transport refrigerators.

  8. Medication

    We only transport personal prescription medication accompanied by pharmacy paperwork or a doctor's authorization.

  9. Pickup receipt

    The person who hands over the load signs a pickup receipt listing the items being transported. The signed receipt is part of the record of your load.

  10. Photos

    We photograph all items at pickup and again at drop-off. These photos are the record of the condition of your items.

  11. Damage and insurance

    If an item arrives damaged, contact us by phone or WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery to report it. Loads are insured up to $2,500 through our Progressive policy.

  12. Additional charges

    Pickups or drop-offs on a 2nd, 3rd or 4th floor, or that require stairs or elevators, have an extra charge of $20 to $30, depending on the job.

  13. Service area

    We serve locations within about 3 hours of Houston, Texas, including cities in Louisiana.
