Two years on the road out of Houston

LLC DCM is a Houston cargo van service. For the past two years we've been moving furniture, appliances and supplies for families and businesses across Texas and Louisiana.

The way we work is simple. We come to your door and photograph everything before it goes in the van. We deliver it the same day, and when we drop it off, we take photos again so you can see it arrived just as it left.

  1. We come to you.

    For personal items, you don't have to bring anything anywhere. We pick up at your home and take it where it needs to go.

  2. Photos at pickup and drop-off.

    You get a clear record of how your items looked before the trip and after it.

  3. You pay once your items are loaded.

    No deposit and no payment when you book. You pay with Zelle or Apple Pay once everything is in the van.

  4. Same-day pickup and delivery.

    Your load never sits in the van overnight. We pick it up and drop it off on the day you choose.

  5. Insured up to $2,500 per load.

    Your cargo is covered through our Progressive policy. If something arrives damaged, call us within 24 hours of delivery.

Questions people ask before booking

How far in advance should I book?

Book 2 to 3 days ahead. That gives us time to plan your route and pickup time.

When do I pay?

Once your items are loaded in the van, right before we head out. We accept Zelle and Apple Pay.

What do I sign?

A pickup receipt listing what you're handing over. Together with the photos we take at pickup and drop-off, it's the record of your load.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at no cost. You haven't paid anything at that point. If you can, let us know at least 24 hours before pickup so we can plan our route.

Do I need to pack my items?

Yes, please have everything packed and ready. Assembled furniture is the exception: we wrap it in moving paper for you.

Do you assemble or take apart furniture?

No. We're a pickup and delivery service, not a full moving company. Please have furniture ready to load.

What if something gets damaged?

Call us and report it within 24 hours of delivery. We photograph every item at pickup and at drop-off, and each load is insured up to $2,500.

Can you transport medication?

Only personal prescription medication, and only with the pharmacy paperwork or your doctor's authorization.

Do you deliver to Louisiana?

Yes. We go to Lafayette and other cities within about 3 hours of Houston.

What are your hours?

Every day, from 8 AM to 8 PM.
